General AI models know a little about everything, but often not enough about your field. We adapt large language models (LLMs) to your industry, your terminology, and your documents, so they deliver precise and reliable answers.

## What We Offer

- Assessment of AI use cases in your company
- Chat assistants based on your own documents and knowledge (retrieval-augmented generation, RAG)
- Fine-tuning of open-source models on your domain data
- Local deployment on your own hardware, so sensitive data does not leave your company
- Integration into existing software and workflows
- Evaluation of answer quality before and after go-live

## How We Work

We start with a small pilot on one concrete use case and measure the results. If the pilot works, we scale it step by step. Data protection and GDPR compliance are part of every project from the start.

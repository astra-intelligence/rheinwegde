Your website is often the first contact a customer has with your company. We build modern, fast websites that present your business clearly, in one language or several.

## What We Offer

- Company websites and landing pages
- Multilingual sites (German, English, Chinese, and more)
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and phone
- Search engine optimization (SEO) basics
- Content management, so you can update texts and images yourself
- Hosting setup, domain, and GDPR-compliant configuration

## How We Work

We start with a short call about your goals and audience. Then we propose structure and design, build the site, and hand it over with a short introduction. Maintenance and updates are available on request.

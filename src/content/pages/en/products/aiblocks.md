An open marketplace for reusable AI components: prompts, skills, subagents, MCP servers, CLAUDE.md configs, hooks, slash commands, Claude plugins, and LLMs. Works across ecosystems (Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek) and also searches the wider web for components not yet in the catalog.

We research Chinese companies according to your requirements, for example by:

- Industry
- Registered capital
- Date of establishment
- Registered business scope
- Registered province
- Listing status
- Number of employees
- Company type

Please tell us your filter criteria, the preferred sorting, and the number of companies you are looking for.

Based on your criteria, we prepare a report from the Chinese company database. The report includes, among other things, the following information on the matching companies (see sample, in German: [RheinWeg-Beispiel-Liste-14-10-2026.xlsx](/downloads/RheinWeg-Beispiel-Liste-14-10-2026.xlsx)):

Company name, business status, unified social credit code, registration number, province, city, district/county, legal representative, registered capital, paid-in capital, date of establishment, approval date, former company names, industry, company type, organization code, number of employees covered by social insurance, business scope, business address, last known address, email address (business information), email address (verification information), shareholders, contact phone numbers (from the commercial register, sorted by recency), further contact numbers (2–10), recommended phone number.

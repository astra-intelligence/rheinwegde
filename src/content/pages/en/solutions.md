## German-China Trade Communication

### 1. Search for Chinese Companies Based on Your Criteria

We research Chinese companies according to your requirements, for example by:

- Industry
- Registered capital
- Date of establishment
- Registered business scope
- Registered province
- Listing status
- Number of employees
- Company type

Please tell us your filter criteria, the preferred sorting, and the number of companies you are looking for.

Based on your criteria, we prepare a report from the Chinese company database. The report includes, among other things, the following information on the matching companies (see sample, in German: [RheinWeg-Beispiel-Liste-22-02-2026.xlsx](/downloads/RheinWeg-Beispiel-Liste-22-02-2026.xlsx)):

Company name, business status, unified social credit code, registration number, province, city, district/county, legal representative, registered capital, paid-in capital, date of establishment, approval date, former company names, industry, company type, organization code, number of employees covered by social insurance, business scope, business address, last known address, email address (business information), email address (verification information), shareholders, contact phone numbers (from the commercial register, sorted by recency), further contact numbers (2–10), recommended phone number.

**Fee:** EUR 1–2 per company, depending on your requirements.

### 2. Detailed Company and Credit Information

If you are interested in detailed information and the creditworthiness of a specific company, we research the following information from public sources, among others (see sample, in German: [RheinWeg-Beispiel-Firma-22-02-2026.xlsx](/downloads/RheinWeg-Beispiel-Firma-22-02-2026.xlsx)):

- Company information
- Shareholder information
- Key personnel
- Annual report 2024
- Number of employees covered by social insurance
- Affiliated companies and employees
- Suspected actual controller
- Ultimate beneficial owner
- Administrative licenses
- Domain registrations
- Historical domain registrations

**Fee:** EUR 20–100 per company, depending on the level of detail required.

### 3. Representation with Chinese Companies

We can support your company in contacting and working with Chinese companies, including:

- Initial contact
- On-site visits
- Contract negotiations
- German–Chinese contract translations
- Organization of the contract signing process
- Support with further contract execution (if required)

**Fee:**

- Before contract signing: free of charge
- At contract signing: 2% of the contract value as a service fee
- After contract signing: further services by separate agreement
